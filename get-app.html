Android

Download for Android (APK)
  1. Tap the button and open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser (Settings > "Install unknown apps"), then tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, choose "More details" > "Install anyway". The app is ours and only downloadable from this page.
  4. Open SCARLS, enter your order email and the 6-digit code we send you.

iPhone & computers

  1. Open scarlssystems.com/app/ in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome.
  2. iPhone: tap Share > Add to Home Screen. Computer: click the install icon in the address bar.
