Our Story

Why SCARLS SYSTEMS exists.

SCARLS SYSTEMS is a digital marketing and growth systems company based in Nigeria, serving businesses locally and across Africa and the world. We started from a simple observation: businesses spend money on marketing and still do not grow, because the pieces are disconnected.

One person runs the ads, another builds the website, nobody sets up tracking, and leads sit unanswered. Each piece may be fine on its own, but together they do not form a system. The business ends up guessing, and growth depends on the founder doing everything by hand.

Our mission is to close the gap between marketing activity and scalable business growth. We design and build the acquisition, conversion and tracking systems that sit underneath a business, so that attracting customers, converting them and measuring the result becomes a repeatable process rather than a gamble.

That is why the name says what it does. Creative work that earns attention. Acquisition that brings in the right people. Revenue as the measure of success. A lifecycle view that looks beyond the first sale. And systems that tie it all together.

What We Do

Five connected layers of growth.

Acquisition

We plan and run paid advertising on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google, with audience research, campaign strategy, lead generation, retargeting and ongoing optimization, so qualified people find your business.

Content and creative

We produce the ad creative, video editing, copy, VSL and webinar content that turns attention into trust, planned around a clear content strategy rather than random posting.

Conversion

We build landing pages, sales pages, lead generation funnels, webinar and VSL funnels, business websites and checkout flows, and then improve them so more visitors become leads and customers.

Tracking

We set up the Meta Pixel, conversion events, analytics and campaign attribution, so you can see which ads, pages and offers actually produce revenue.

Scale

We use what the data shows to refine campaigns and funnels, and to put more budget and effort behind what works, so growth becomes repeatable.

How We Think

What we believe about growth.

Systems over one-off tasks

A single ad, page or post rarely changes a business. We connect acquisition, conversion, tracking and follow-up so each part makes the others work better.

Revenue is the scoreboard

Clicks and likes are not the goal. We design every campaign and funnel around leads, sales and the cost of getting them.

Measure before you scale

We set up conversion tracking first, so decisions come from data, and money only goes into what has proven it works.

Clear prices, plain language

Where a service has a fixed scope we publish the price. Where it does not, we scope it with you in plain language before you pay.

Built for African businesses

We understand local realities: Naira budgets, mobile-first customers, WhatsApp-driven sales and the payment methods people actually use.

Independence, not dependence

We document and build systems so the business can keep growing without relying on any one person, including us.
